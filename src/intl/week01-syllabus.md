**Computer Science 2** — Fall Semester 2026, Course Code MC1202, 2 credits,
Lecture, 2 hours/week.

## Course Descriptions

- Learn introductory-level Python syntax/semantics
- Practice building small-sized programs in Python
- Solve elementary algorithmic problems with Python

## Core Target Competencies

- **Fundamental Programming and Problem-Solving Skills** — understand basic
  Python syntax and apply it to analyze and solve problems logically
- **Logical Thinking and Analytical Skills** — analyze problems using
  structured, algorithmic thinking
- **Communication and Collaboration Skills** — explain concepts clearly,
  listen actively to peers
- **Ethical Use of AI and Responsible Attitude** — behave ethically and
  responsibly when using AI tools for assignments and class activities

## Evaluation

| In-class Problems & Quiz | Midterm | Final | Attendance & Attitude | Sum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 30 | 30 | 10 | 100 |

Absolute evaluation. In-class problems each session. Midterm and final are
2-hour written exams. Pass requires 70/100 or higher, and at least 3/4 of
total class hours attended. Excused/medical absences use a weighted
make-up score (100% official, 80% medical) based on the student's
percentile in other assessments.
